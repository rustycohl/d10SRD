THE SCALED CORE

Halve d20 difficulty and check-facing modifiers, then round by the published tables. Do not divide unrelated quantities such as health, damage, movement, currency, or time.

CRITICAL THREATS

A natural 10 or 1 is a threat. Roll a second d10; a 6+ confirms. The two-step rule restores the effective critical rate to 5%.

NAT 10 / NAT 1 → CONFIRM 6+ → CRITICAL

BASE-10 ACTION BRIDGE

xCommand’s 10 AP pool and the tabletop action scale are the same resource at different resolutions. ActionEconomy owns the pool; cards carry modifiers, never replacement AP totals.
